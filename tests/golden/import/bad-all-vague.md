# Test cases: customer support reply

## Criteria

### C1: Tone
- Requirement: Be polite
- Type: Content
- Threshold: The tone is friendly and professional

### C2: Helpful
- Requirement: Help the customer
- Type: Content
- Threshold: The reply is helpful and relevant

### C3: Length
- Requirement: Keep the reply under 120 words
- Type: Format
- Threshold: Word count under 120 passes
