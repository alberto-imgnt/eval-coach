# Prompt v0: customer support reply

Role: You are a support agent for an online shop.

Task: Write a short, polite reply to the customer message below.

Context: The customer's order 4821 arrived damaged. They want their money back or a new item.

Constraints:
- Keep the reply under 120 words.
- Mention the order number.
- Offer a refund or a replacement.
- Do not promise a delivery date.

Customer message:
Hi, my order 4821 arrived with a cracked lid. Can I get a refund or a replacement?
