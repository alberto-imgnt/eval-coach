# Typical gaps in a first prompt

These are the gaps people typically notice when they compare a first answer with the five things they knew but never said. They are examples, not other people's data. People will not always agree on which kind a gap is. What matters is that every gap fits somewhere, and that each one was something the prompt never said. The five kinds are an example, not a complete list: if a gap fits none of them, it points to a kind you may need to add.

## Five common kinds of missing information

1. Who the AI should act as
2. What exactly to produce
3. Background it needs
4. Limits and format
5. What a good result looks like

## Typical gaps and where they fit

| Gap people noticed | Kind of missing information | Why it fits there |
|---|---|---|
| Sounds like I'm bragging | 1. Who the AI should act as | The prompt never said whose voice to write in or how that person sounds. |
| Reads like a formal report, not a quick note | 1. Who the AI should act as | The prompt never said who is writing, so the AI chose a voice. |
| An email with a subject line, not a Teams message | 2. What exactly to produce | The prompt never said what kind of thing to produce. |
| Didn't know who the update was for | 3. Background it needs | The reader and the situation were in your head, not in the prompt. |
| Left out that I work on two clients plus internal work | 3. Background it needs | The AI had no way to know how your week is organized. |
| Way over 150 words | 4. Limits and format | The prompt set no length. |
| Went Monday to Friday instead of by client | 4. Limits and format | The prompt never gave the order or layout. |
| Blocker buried and no clear ask | 5. What a good result looks like | The prompt never said what the update must make clear. |
| Never said what I need from my lead | 5. What a good result looks like | The prompt never said what a useful update ends with. |

## Gaps to set aside for now

Some things people notice are real but are not about missing information. They belong to evaluation in later Parts.

| Gap people noticed | Why it is set aside |
|---|---|
| Made up a detail about Contoso | An invented detail is a question of accuracy, which later Parts check. |
| Just felt boring | Overall quality is a judgment, which later Parts turn into clear rules. |
