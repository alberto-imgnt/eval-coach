# Sample prompt: RISE labels

This is one way to write the Friday update with RISE labels. It is a sample, not the right answer. Compare it with the RTCC sample for the same task.

```
Role: You are a consultant writing a quick Friday update to your project lead.

Input: My rough notes from this week are below. This goes to my project lead in Teams.
- Mon: standup and two Northwind client meetings about the new reporting feature
- Mon–Tue: worked on the Northwind reporting feature
- Wed: helped onboard a new teammate
- Wed: prepared and ran the Northwind demo
- Thu: blocked for half a day waiting on client test data access; worked on documentation instead
- Thu: attended the internal AI lunch & learn
- Fri: fixed issues from demo feedback; reviewed a Contoso estimate

Steps:
1. Group the work by client: Northwind, Contoso, Internal.
2. Summarize each group in one or two short bullets.
3. End with a "Blocker" line: what is blocking me and what I need to resolve it.

Expectation: A Teams message, not an email, under 150 words, with no subject line. Factual, not self-promotional.
```
