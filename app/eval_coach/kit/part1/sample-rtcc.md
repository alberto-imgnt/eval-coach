# Sample prompt: RTCC labels

This is one way to write the Friday update with RTCC labels. It is a sample, not the right answer. Compare it with the RISE sample for the same task.

```
Role: You are a consultant writing a quick Friday update to your project lead.

Task: Turn my rough notes below into a short end-of-week update, sent as a Teams message.

Context: This goes to my project lead. I work on two client accounts, Northwind and Contoso, plus some internal work. I was blocked this week waiting for Northwind's test data access.

Constraints: Under 150 words. No subject line. Group the work by client (Northwind, Contoso, Internal), not by day. Call out the blocker and what I need to resolve it. Factual tone, not self-promotional.

Notes:
- Mon: standup and two Northwind client meetings about the new reporting feature
- Mon–Tue: worked on the Northwind reporting feature
- Wed: helped onboard a new teammate
- Wed: prepared and ran the Northwind demo
- Thu: blocked for half a day waiting on client test data access; worked on documentation instead
- Thu: attended the internal AI lunch & learn
- Fri: fixed issues from demo feedback; reviewed a Contoso estimate
```
