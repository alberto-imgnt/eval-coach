# Five example kinds of information and two sample frameworks

People have named kinds of information a prompt needs. A prompting framework is a checklist of those names, so you do not forget one. The five kinds below are an example from this course. They are common, but they are not a complete list, and other kinds exist. The two frameworks are samples, shown side by side. They are not recommendations.

| What the AI needs | RTCC | RISE |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Who the AI should act as | Role | Role |
| 2. What exactly to produce | Task | Steps |
| 3. Background it needs | Context | Input |
| 4. Limits and format | Constraints | Expectation (partly) |
| 5. What a good result looks like | Constraints (partly) | Expectation |

## How to read the table

- RTCC stands for Role, Task, Context, Constraints. RISE stands for Role, Input, Steps, Expectation. These are common expansions, and other sources define the letters differently.
- Kinds 4 and 5 fit only partly. RTCC puts both limits and a good result under Constraints. RISE puts both under Expectation. Each framework leans on some kinds more than others.
- Both frameworks cover all five example kinds when they are filled in well. Covering what your task needs matters more than the letters.

## Others exist, and you can build your own

CO-STAR and RISEN are two more well-known frameworks. Search for prompting frameworks and you will find many others, and each picks its own kinds.

The five kinds here are only the ones this course uses as an example. Your task may need kinds that are not on this list, for example which sources to use, what to do when the AI is unsure, or examples of a good result. When no framework fits, build your own labels: start from these five and add the kinds your task needs, or drop the ones it does not. What matters is that your prompt gives the AI what it needs.

## Skeletons

Fill in each label with your own words.

RTCC:

```
Role: who the AI acts as
Task: what to produce
Context: the background it needs
Constraints: limits and format
```

RISE:

```
Role: who the AI acts as
Input: your rough notes, plus the background it needs
Steps: how to work through it
Expectation: what a good result looks like
```
