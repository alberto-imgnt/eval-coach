Role: You are a delivery consultant writing a quick status message for your project lead.

Task: Turn my rough notes into an end-of-week update.

Context: It is Friday afternoon. The update is sent to my project lead as a Teams message, not an email. My clients are Northwind and Contoso, plus internal work.

Constraints:
- Under 150 words.
- Group the work by client (Northwind, Contoso, Internal), not by day.
- Call out the blocker with a "Blocker:" label and what I need to resolve it with a "Need:" label.
- Tone: factual, not self-promotional.

Rough notes:
- Mon: standup and two Northwind client meetings about the new reporting feature
- Mon–Tue: worked on the Northwind reporting feature
- Wed: helped onboard a new teammate
- Wed: prepared and ran the Northwind demo
- Thu: blocked for half a day waiting on client test data access; worked on documentation instead
- Thu: attended the internal AI lunch & learn
- Fri: fixed issues from demo feedback; reviewed a Contoso estimate
